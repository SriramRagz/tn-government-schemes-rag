
Q: What is the purpose of the Tamil Nadu Agriculture and Farmers Welfare Department schemes?
A: The schemes support agricultural development and farmer welfare through activities such as agricultural inputs, technology adoption, crop production support, farmer services and sustainable farming. The Tamil Nadu Agriculture Department publishes scheme descriptions, subsidy information, eligibility conditions and required documents on its official schemes portal. Scheme benefits depend on the individual scheme and its current guidelines. Source: https://www.tnagriculture.in/Agritech_new/home/schemes/tm

Q: What is Uzhavar Aluvalar Thodarbu Thittam (UATT)?
A: Uzhavar Aluvalar Thodarbu Thittam is listed as an agricultural activity on the Tamil Nadu Agriculture Department's 2026–27 schemes portal. Farmers can refer to the department's official portal or contact the Agriculture Department for current implementation details, eligibility and available services. Source: https://www.tnagriculture.in/Agritech_new/home/schemes/tm

Q: What is the Collective Farming scheme in Tamil Nadu?
A: Collective Farming aims to bring small and marginal farmers together into Farmer Interest Groups and support their development into Farmer Producer Organisations. Its objectives include sharing agricultural knowledge, improving access to cost-effective technologies, supporting group development and improving access to credit and markets. Source: https://madurai.nic.in/agriculture/

Q: What is the Tamil Nadu Mission for Sustainable Dry Land Development?
A: The Tamil Nadu Agriculture Department lists the Tamil Nadu Mission for Sustainable Dry Land Development (TNMSDD) among its 2026–27 schemes. The district agriculture information describes sustainable dryland agriculture activities such as summer ploughing, agronomic interventions, water-harvesting structures and support for value-added machinery. Farmers should contact their local Agriculture Department office for current scheme components and eligibility. Source: https://www.tnagriculture.in/Agritech_new/home/schemes/tm

Q: What is the purpose of the Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)?
A: Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana provides crop insurance support to farmers against specified crop losses. The Government of Tamil Nadu district agriculture information describes coverage for prevented sowing, standing crop losses caused by events such as drought, floods, inundation, pests and diseases, certain post-harvest losses and specified localised calamities. Coverage and claim conditions depend on the notified crop, area and season. Source: https://madurai.nic.in/agriculture/

Q: What is a Soil Health Card and how does it help farmers?
A: The Soil Health Card initiative aims to help farmers understand soil fertility conditions and receive soil-test-based fertiliser recommendations. The Madurai District Agriculture Department describes issuing soil health cards periodically and using soil test results to support better nutrient management. Source: https://madurai.nic.in/agriculture/

Q: What is the purpose of micro-irrigation under Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana?
A: The micro-irrigation component of Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana promotes efficient use of irrigation water through systems such as drip irrigation and rain guns. Its objective is to conserve water and improve crop productivity. Farmers should check the current horticulture department guidelines for applicable assistance and eligibility. Source: https://madurai.nic.in/agriculture/

Q: What services are available through the Tamil Nadu Uzhavan mobile application?
A: The Uzhavan application provides farmer services including farmer benefit registration, subsidy scheme information, crop insurance details, fertiliser and seed stock information, agricultural machinery custom hiring centre information, daily market prices and district-wise weather forecasts. Availability of individual services may depend on the relevant department and scheme. Source: https://tnagriculture.in/people_app/

Q: Where can farmers find official Tamil Nadu agricultural scheme information?
A: Farmers can use the Tamil Nadu Agriculture Department's official schemes portal to look for scheme descriptions, subsidy information, eligibility conditions and required documents. They can also contact the Agriculture Department at Chepauk, Chennai. The department portal lists the contact telephone number as 044-28583323. Farmers should verify current scheme details with the relevant department before applying. Source: https://www.tnagriculture.in/Agritech_new/home/schemes/tm
